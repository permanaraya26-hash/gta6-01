Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty [Make GTA 6](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01) — $1,666 USDC total, top 100 paid.

**Solmar Bay** is a complete open-world crime game that plays offline in a desktop browser from static files: a procedural coastal city to explore on foot and by car, traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal and drive, a 0–5 star wanted level with pursuing police, pistol and fist combat, a 3-mission story for fixer Vera Cruz, a full HUD with live minimap, start/pause menus and a localStorage save.

Open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome — no network, no build step. `main.ts` hands the game in: it copies `game/` and `screenshots/` to `/output` and writes `/output/dossier.json`.
